6. Settings & notifications

Profile, filter memory, local notifications, sign out.

Profile

Shows your name, email, and photo when available. Tap the pencil to edit the name (at least 2 characters). The name updates in Auth, your profile, and the family member list.

Remember task filters

  • On — saves view + status; default login often For me + To Do
  • Off — Tasks opens with All + All statuses

Haptic feedback

  • On (default) — light vibration on Done; medium on Delete
  • Off — no vibration from the app for those actions

Preference is saved per account. Turning the switch on gives a short pulse to confirm.

App lock

  • On — set a 4-digit PIN; optional biometrics (fingerprint / face)
  • Unlock required after opening the app or returning from background
  • Unlock with biometrics — when off, only PIN
  • Change PIN — confirm with biometrics or PIN first
  • Turning lock off requires confirmation (biometrics or PIN)

Google sign-in is unchanged. App lock only protects access on this device.

Notify about new tasks

1
Toggle on May request system notification permission.
Local alerts: New-task notifications appear after this device syncs (app open / login). They are not full cloud push while the app never runs.

When someone else creates a Family task or assigns a Personal task to you, you may get New task for you or New family task. Tap opens task details.

Sign-in methods

One account can use both Google and email (same user id).

  • Link next to Google — connect a Google account (if you registered with email)
  • Add next to Email and password — set email + password (if you signed in with Google)

If that Google/email already belongs to another FamilyTasks user, linking is refused. Sign in with that account instead.

Notification permission

Separate row to request Android permission again if needed.

Help

Opens this user manual inside the app (English / Russian). Works offline.

Reminders

Task form presets “In 1 hour” / “In 1 day” schedule local reminders (channel: Task reminders).

Sign out

2
Tap Sign out Returns to login. Session ends on this device.
