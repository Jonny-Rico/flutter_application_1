Install, sign in (Google or email), and learn the main tabs.

Sign in

Google Tap Sign in with Google and pick an account. Cancel closes the picker without error.
3
Email Sign in with email or Create account (name, email, password ≥ 8). Forgot password? sends a reset link.
4
You land on Tasks Bottom navigation appears. Personal tasks work immediately. Email users must verify the inbox link before creating or joining a family.
